# Site audit, 4 November 2026

The whole site, on commit 4d111b6. Four read-only passes: security and privacy, database correctness, a live crawl
(360px and 1280px, every sitemap URL, axe-core) and copy against code. Then type check, a production build with no
secrets, and `npm audit`.

What was live when the audit ran: 12 approved stories, all of them 1937 examples. The 6 real submissions are all
removed. No family's story is public yet.

**How each item is judged.** Every fix has to rest on something the site already promises, or on the law. The
promises are in three places:

- **About:** the About page (`src/app/about/page.tsx`), under "What we collect" and "Privacy and consent".
- **Consent:** the consent box (`src/lib/i18n.ts`, `consent`).
- **DPIA:** `docs/data-protection.md`, which is also what schools sign.

"Basis" says which promise or rule an item rests on. "Status" says what was done. An item with no basis is dropped,
not fixed.

## Healthy

- **Build and deploy:** the type check is clean, and the production build passes with no secrets. The live deploy matches master, with start.sh, draining and the health check in place.
- **Crawl:** about 300 URLs, none broken.
- **Unpublished content:** nothing leaks through a public page or API.
