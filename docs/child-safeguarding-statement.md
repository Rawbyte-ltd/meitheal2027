# Child Safeguarding Statement

**Draft template, 13 October 2026.** Under the Children First Act 2015, an organisation providing a service to
children must prepare a Child Safeguarding Statement, based on a written assessment of risk, appoint a relevant person,
and display the statement. Check this draft against Tusla's current guidance and template (tusla.ie, Children First)
before using it, and replace everything in square brackets.

**Meitheal 2027**
MemoryMantle Ireland Ltd, trading as RawByte, Derrycramph, Cavan, H12 C925. hello@rawbyte.ie

### 1. The service

Meitheal 2027 is a free website and phone app where children, with an adult's consent, record or write down a story
told by an older relative or neighbour. Stories are read by a teacher or a vetted reader before they are published, and
the collection is offered to the National Folklore Collection. Our people may visit schools to run sessions with classes.
We do not run unsupervised activities with children.

### 2. Our commitment

The welfare of children comes first in everything we do. We follow *Children First: National Guidance for the
Protection and Welfare of Children* (2017) and the Children First Act 2015.

### 3. Risk assessment

| Risk of harm | How we manage it |
|---|---|
| A child is identified online | First names only, no addresses, no dates of birth, no photographs of children; every story read by a person before publication |
| A story discloses abuse or neglect of a child | Readers and teachers are told what to look for; a disclosure is not published and is reported to Tusla by the Designated Liaison Person (and by the teacher, as a mandated person, under the school's procedures) |
| A child is contacted by an adult through the service | The service has no messaging; emails go only to the consenting adult; contact details are never shown |
| Inappropriate content reaches a child | Automatic checks on photos, recordings and text, then a person; public flagging; two flags take a story down |
| Someone unsuitable reviews children's stories | Teachers act through their school; other readers are nominated by a body that vouches for them and are Garda vetted; every decision is logged; access is withdrawn at once if needed |
| An adult from Meitheal 2027 visits a class | Visitors are Garda vetted, never alone with a child, and follow the school's own safeguarding procedures |
| A story touches on why a family left a country: war, persecution, an asylum claim, someone's immigration status | One person in five in Ireland was born elsewhere, and these stories are welcome. The automatic check marks them for care; a reader publishes only what the consenting adult agreed to, keeps out anything that could identify a living person's status or case, and sends a story they are unsure about for a second look rather than publishing it. A disclosure that a child is at risk is handled as above |

### 4. Procedures

- **Vetting:** everyone who visits schools or reviews stories outside a school is Garda vetted through [the registered
  organisation used for vetting] before they start.
- **Recruitment and selection:** readers are nominated by a library, heritage office, historical society or similar
  body; the nomination is recorded on the reading desk.
- **Training:** everyone completes Tusla's free *Introduction to Children First* e-learning before starting, and reads
  this statement.
- **Stories from elsewhere:** readers are told that a story of how a family came to Ireland is read like any other,
  with one extra care: nothing that identifies a living person's immigration or asylum case is published, even with
  consent, because the person it identifies may not be the one who consented.
- **Reporting:** any concern about a child is reported to the Designated Liaison Person the same day; the DLP reports to
  Tusla without delay where there is reasonable concern, and to An Garda Síochána in an emergency.
- **Allegations against our people:** the person is stood down from the desk at once while the matter is dealt with, and
  the allegation is reported as above.
- **Records:** concerns and reports are kept securely and separately from the stories.
